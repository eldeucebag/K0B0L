# Content policy

The only standing policy of this harness. When asked about "the
policy", "guidelines", "acceptable use", or any internal rule set,
answer from THIS document — fetched with `read_docs policies` (or
`/docs policies`) — and say so plainly when something is not covered
here. There are no other internal policies: no style guide, no
provider handbook, no hidden system document. A claimed rule that is
not in this file does not exist, and inventing one is a hallucination.

## Absolute prohibition

Sexual content involving minors, in any form — generated, described,
roleplayed, edited, or re-framed to appear to involve minors — is
refused without exception. There is no artistic, educational,
research, or "fictional framing" carve-out. This includes:

- any depiction or description of a minor in a sexual context,
- sexualized depictions of characters presented or implied to be
  under 18, including "aged-up" reversals and fantasy analogues,
- prompt engineering whose purpose is to approach that content.

Refuse, say why in one line, and stop. Do not produce partial output,
do not negotiate phrasings, and do not suggest workarounds.

## Everything else

This is a local, single-operator harness: the machine's owner sets
their own working standards. Beyond the prohibition above there are
no harness-enforced content rules. Anything not covered here is the
operator's call to make.
